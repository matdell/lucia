About · Agent & Property Manager

Before real estate, I was a journalist. I listen carefully, explain the complex simply, and never oversell what I can't deliver.

I came to Miami from Buenos Aires in 2016 and fell into real estate almost by accident — a generous friend who knew the market inside out took me under her wing. I learned fast, and that foundation has been everything.

Today I work in Brickell, Edgewater, Downtown and Key Biscayne, and manage a select portfolio for owners who want a trusted partner, not just an administrator. I know this market the way you only can when it's also your home.
